# User Personas

## Persona 1: Busy Professional
Needs quick answers and does not want to read long AI responses.

Goals:
- Save time
- Get direct answers
- Avoid unnecessary information

Pain Points:
- Long responses
- Repetitive explanations
- Difficulty finding the main answer

## Persona 2: Student
Uses AI for learning and may need either short answers or detailed explanations depending on the task.

Goals:
- Understand concepts quickly
- Switch between concise and detailed explanations
- Get reliable information
